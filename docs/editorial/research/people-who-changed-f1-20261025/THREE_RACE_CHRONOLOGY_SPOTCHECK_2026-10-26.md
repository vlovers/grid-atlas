# Three race chronologies — source spot-check, 26 October 2026

**Status:** source-backed editorial working note only. Not independent approval or publication clearance. The linked pages do not grant photo or footage rights.

## Montreal 2011 — Button

- F1 retrospective: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/canada-2011-torrential-rain-a-safety-car-record-and-buttons-charge-the.1ZWPateMlCFLZ8U6KQBNXj
- F1 long-form race account: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/f1s-best-drives-9-buttons-against-the-odds-canadian-comeback.5KWCMW2CEMQwbtzJcAA8Jh
- Button changed to slick tyres on lap 51; Heidfeld/Kobayashi contact prompted another Safety Car on lap 56. Button capitalised on Webber's error at the final chicane on lap 64, passed Schumacher on lap 65, and inherited the lead when Vettel erred on the final lap.
- Editorial caveat: avoid implying the driver had a continuous unbroken march forward; race included stoppage, Safety Cars and reversals.

## Interlagos 2008 — Massa/Hamilton/Glock

- F1 championship retrospective: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/9-thrilling-title-deciding-finales-from-f1-history.57lx35G8vIsWRCOL5LV6cF.57lx35G8vIsWRCOL5LV6cF
- F1 onboard and Glock's retrospective explanation: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/f1-classic-onboard-glock-brazil-2008-final-lap.4LNMAO7WxtLnE38uvEURtg.4LNMAO7WxtLnE38uvEURtg
- Massa led 64 of 71 laps and won. Hamilton lost fifth to Vettel late, then recovered P5 past slick-shod Glock as rain intensified at the end, winning the title by one point.
- Editorial caveat: reject conspiracy claims or inferred motives. Tyre differences and finishing order are supported; no unverified radio transcripts.

## Suzuka 1989 — Senna/Prost/Nannini

- F1 classification: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1989/races/557/japan/race-result
- F1 period retrospective: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/moments-in-time-the-japanese-grand-prix.5M4HpgAHNmMjn15iW7nAXO
- F1 disqualification account: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/from-infamous-title-deciders-to-home-race-heartbreaks-7-of-the-most-dramatic.3gUysRekRcxhLJ1MgzvFDZ
- Collision between McLaren teammates at chicane, Prost stopped; Senna rejoined, replaced his front wing and passed Nannini on track. Senna was later disqualified for cutting the chicane when rejoining, handing official victory to Nannini and the drivers' title to Prost.
- **Unresolved discrepancy:** F1 retrospective pages differ in lap designation (lap 46 vs lap 47), likely due to lap-counting conventions. Do not insert an exact collision lap until cross-checked with the original timing/ruling.
- Editorial caveat: motive and blame for the collision remain contested; do not equate the collision itself with the rule invoked in the classification.
