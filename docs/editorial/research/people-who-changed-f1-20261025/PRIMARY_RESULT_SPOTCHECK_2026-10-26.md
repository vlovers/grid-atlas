## Senna / Prost — 1988

  Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1988/drivers
- Official Formula 1 race-winner listing for all 16 rounds: **McLaren Honda won 15 of 16**; **Ferrari / Gerhard Berger won Italy**.
  Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1988/races
- The official standings page presents *counted championship points*, **not** Prost's claimed 105 raw or Senna's claimed 94 raw. These raw totals and the eleven-best-results regulation still require a separately recorded scoring-rule verification before being treated as independently checked in the editorial ledger.

## Massa / Hamilton — Brazil 2008

- Official Formula 1 race classification (2 November 2008): **Massa P1, Vettel P4, Hamilton P5, Glock P6**, all classified after 71 laps.
  Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2008/races/842/brazil/race-result
- Formula 1's historical rules retrospective records the actual final points: **Hamilton 98; Massa 97**.
  Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/history-re-written-how-fastest-lap-points-would-have-swung-two-key.4M4gV6GjUMhnoeNhi18rsY
- The classification alone **does not establish** tyre choices, when overtakes occurred, Massa's exact laps led, why Glock slowed or any driver's private intentions. Those narrative details must retain their separate source requirements.
- The historic 2008 scoring system must not be silently replaced with today's points system.
