**EN / Explore.** Rain made the result possible to imagine; it did not drive the car. The surprising part is not only that a midfield team found pace on Saturday, but that its young driver converted the opportunity on Sunday. The story must distinguish the Toro Rosso constructor from Red Bull Racing and avoid describing a later corporate lineage as a verified archive relationship.

**EN / Explore.** The winning pass should not be told as an isolated miracle. Changing conditions created repeated strategic decisions, while Safety Cars compressed the gaps. Button still had to make the passes after the restart. The final-lap mistake by Vettel provided the opening, not the entire explanation for the comeback.

**UK / Коротко.** Гран-прі Канади 2011 року тривав понад чотири години через сильну зливу та зупинку перегонів. Баттон пережив зіткнення, штраф і шість заїздів на пітлейн. Він опинявся позаду всіх, але повернувся в боротьбу й наздогнав Феттеля на останньому колі.
