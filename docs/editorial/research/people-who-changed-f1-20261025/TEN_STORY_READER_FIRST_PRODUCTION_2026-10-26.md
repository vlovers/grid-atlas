## Cross-story QA and acceptance

1. **Opening:** each story starts with a particular season, race or choice; no vague “this changed F1 forever” claims.
2. **What happened:** results and standings have a designated primary locator and explicit event year; no false equivalence between an exciting battle and a race victory.
3. **Why it matters:** the interpretation remains clearly separated from the classified result, especially Lauda/Hunt, Kubica's title hopes, Russell's unrealized chance, and the role of weather at Monza.
4. **Continuity:** 1988 counted points are not the same as raw points; Toro Rosso is not Red Bull Racing; a constructor is not automatically an entrant/ownership lineage.
5. **Readability:** one result explanation, one genuinely distinct new development per chapter, no repeated concluding moral in every section. Avoid empty numbered cards and backend-language in reader UI.
6. **Parity:** both EN and UK must tell the same factual story with idiomatic independent prose, not translated boilerplate.
7. **Photography:** source-page availability and a claimed Creative Commons label do not mean rights-approved media. No photos until a complete reviewed local asset with verified bytes and attribution exists.
8. **Release:** do not merge this draft as a public historical article until independent reviewer, date, exact passages, and source URLs are in the claim ledger. Technical CI success alone is insufficient.
