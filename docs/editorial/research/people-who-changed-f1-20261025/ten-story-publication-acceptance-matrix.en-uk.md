# Grid Atlas — ten-story publication acceptance matrix

**Status:** Unpublished editorial acceptance checklist. This is an execution artifact, not proof of completed verification or implemented routes. Every record requires EN and UK editorial approval.

## Acceptance gates

A story is publishable only after: (1) fact claims checked against actual classifications and reliable contemporary material; (2) each language independently edited; (3) entity IDs resolved from the pinned archive; (4) artwork provenance cleared; (5) story rendered in the application and keyboard/mobile tested; (6) CI passes. A research URL is not a verified citation.
