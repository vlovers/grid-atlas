Status: source spot-check, **not independent historical/editorial approval**. The following source claims may guide revised scenes in the unpublished EN/UK manuscripts. Source URLs are explicit; no race footage or images are licensed by these links.

## Vettel, Monza 2008

Official 2008 Italian Grand Prix classification: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2008/races/838/italy/race-result

- Vettel classified first in the `STR Ferrari` entry, ahead of Heikki Kovalainen and Robert Kubica.
- Race distance in the official classification: 53 laps; margin over Kovalainen: 12.512 seconds.
- `STR Ferrari` refers to Toro Rosso's constructor entry and engine designation, **not Scuderia Ferrari or Red Bull Racing**.
- The classification verifies the finishing order and gap, not pole, pit-stop lap timings, or the motivation behind strategy choices. Those details need independent event reporting.

## Pérez, Sakhir 2020

Contemporary Formula 1 race report: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/sergio-perez-takes-sensational-maiden-win-in-sakhir-gp-as-tyre-issues-ruin.2TBf7rg5wpgELNALXJLRHV

F1 report on the stewards' tyre decision: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/mercedes-handed-eur20-000-fine-for-russell-tyre-mix-up-in-sakhir-grand-prix.3PwKTGxftR3C8ACA5DcDKj

- Pérez pitted after contact on lap one and rejoined last (P18 in the report).
- The Mercedes pit-stop tyre mix-up began with the lap-62 Safety Car sequence. Russell received tyres allocated to Bottas, then had to pit again.
- A subsequent puncture destroyed Russell's second charge. He finished P9; the report notes his first F1 points.
- Pérez won on his 190th Formula 1 start; Ocon finished second, Stroll third.
- The source reports Pérez up to P11 on lap 15 and P10 by lap 20. These are useful intermediate beats if incorporated with equal specificity in both languages.
- The stewards' separate report attributes the wrong-tyre episode to radio communication problems and describes the sanction. Do not turn that into an invented exchange or suggest deliberate sabotage.
- Neither source supports a guaranteed counterfactual Russell victory. Keep the result distinct from an interpretation of his lost opportunity.
