# Ayrton Senna — Monaco 1984: first podium research card

**Status:** DRAFT / research only (2026-10-20). Neither locale is approved or publishable. This is separate from the Monaco wins card: Senna was **second** in 1984, not the race winner.

## English — proposed copy

At the Monaco Grand Prix on 3 June 1984, Ayrton Senna finished second for Toleman-Hart behind Alain Prost of McLaren-TAG. Senna had started thirteenth. The official classification records 31 laps for both and a 7.446-second gap between the winner and Senna. This was Senna's first Formula One podium according to the historical season account; that career-first assertion needs a separate review of earlier classifications.
