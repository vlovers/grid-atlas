# Ayrton Senna — six Monaco Grand Prix victories (research card)

**Editorial status:** DRAFT / research only, 2026-10-20. Not approved for publication. Independent human checks required in both languages.

## English — candidate encyclopedia copy

Ayrton Senna won the Monaco Grand Prix six times: in 1987 and in five consecutive seasons from 1989 to 1993. His final Monaco victory came on 23 May 1993, driving for McLaren-Ford. The official race classification places Senna first ahead of Damon Hill and Jean Alesi; the starting-grid record places him third.
