## Baseline (recalculated at `f0641a50`, F1DB v2026.17.0)

- 78 circuits, 160 layouts, **2,550 numbered turns across all layouts**. Drafted corner guides cover one layout for 69 circuits (977 turns). Of those, 182 carry a recorded name, 486 are number-only and 309 are research-incomplete; 1,573 turns on other layouts have never been researched. **0 verified.** (The earlier figure of 218 named sections includes 36 whose numbering is itself unverified.)
- 5 layouts have race-level turn counts that disagree with the layout record (`conflicting_evidence`).
- 382 existing content blocks: 292 FACT-CHECK, 90 EXPAND, 0 KEEP; **382/382 fail the publication policy**, mainly for lack of extracted claims, evidence and independent review. 17 blocks carry a significant number (year, decimal or value ≥ 21) in only one language — candidate factual discrepancies.
- **0/78 content complete, 0/78 media ready, 0/78 publication eligible, 0/78 production verified.**
- Missing everywhere: timelines, origins, safety-change and present-day sections; reasons for every layout change; claim-level evidence.

## Status vocabulary

Statuses describe evidence, never effort. A status only advances when the evidence that defines it is recorded.

- **Section status:** `absent` → `draft_unverified` → `fact_checked` → `reviewed` (EN and UK separately).
- **Detail evidence class (layouts, identity):** `verified` (dataset plus an independent source, or two independent sources) · `single_source_dataset` / `single_credible_source` · `conflicting_evidence` · `unknown` · `not_applicable`.
- **Corner status:** `officially_named` · `commonly_named` · `historically_named` · `number_only` · `name_disputed` · `research_incomplete` · `not_researched`. Current data only supports `named_unverified`, `number_only_unverified`, `research_incomplete` and `not_researched`; none reaches a verified class until claim-level evidence exists.
- **Triage of existing blocks:** KEEP · IMPROVE · EXPAND · FACT-CHECK · REWRITE · REMOVE · NOT APPLICABLE. The generator applies only deterministic signals (missing language, untranslated text, boilerplate, near-duplicates, short length, EN/UK number drift); KEEP is never assigned without evidence-backed review.

## Editorial acceptance standard

### Content complete (all required)

1. Identity verified: canonical, historical and local names; country and place; location — each with evidence class `verified`.
2. Core statistics match the pinned dataset (races, winners, poles, fastest laps, first/last appearance); differences from historical sources are documented, not silently corrected.
3. Every recorded layout is accounted for: seasons, length, turns, direction, change from the previous layout, and the reason for the change or an explicit `unknown`.
4. Corners researched to the extent evidence allows: each turn of each relevant layout has a status; modern names are never applied to earlier layouts without evidence; no invented names, speeds, braking points, lines, coordinates, elevation, lap records or quotations.
5. Known uncertainty recorded in the text and the data.
6. Major history covered in proportion to the venue: obscure one-race venues stay short; Monaco, Monza or Silverstone get depth. No padding to hit a length.
7. Original, specific prose: no generic corner text ("requires precision and concentration"), no stacked boilerplate, no near-duplicates of other circuits.
8. English and Ukrainian complete, natural and independently audited. Ukrainian is written, not transliterated word for word. Years, decimals and turn numbers stay numerals in both languages so they can be checked; write full years ("1982", not "'82") and integers without thousands separators.
9. Unsupported claims removed or explicitly qualified.
10. Every non-trivial claim traces to evidence (verbatim excerpt from a stored source, or a pinned dataset record).
11. Independent review passes under the publication policy.
12. Required sections render in both locales (CI receipt bound to the content digest).

**Content complete ≠ media ready ≠ publication eligible ≠ production verified.** A missing photograph never makes verified text incomplete; an HTTP 200 never counts towards content.

### Modular page structure

Each circuit is a set of independent blocks the design system can compose differently: 1 introduction · 2 essential verified facts · 3 origins and early history · 4 Formula 1 history · 5 defining moments · 6 layout evolution · 7 corners and track character · 8 important races · 9 records and statistics · 10 legacy and present-day status · 11 related drivers and constructors · 12 sources and methodology. A block may be legitimately absent (`not_applicable`) and must then say so rather than be filled.

## Prioritization

`priority_score = 0.4·importance + 0.3·current-calendar + 0.2·reusable material − 0.1·remaining corner effort`: importance is race count relative to Monza; reusable material combines existing research documents, long drafts and drafted guides; effort is unresearched plus incomplete turns. Ranks are in `circuits.json`. Low-ranked historical venues stay in the queue; rank sets order, never exclusion.
