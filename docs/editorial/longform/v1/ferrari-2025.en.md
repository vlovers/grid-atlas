# Ferrari's SF-25: a new car and a new pairing

> AI-authored research feature · Source-linked, not independently reviewed · Not a published article

## A February reveal

On 18 February 2025, Formula 1 reported the first images of Ferrari's new challenger, the SF-25. The date matters: this is a snapshot of the car as presented ahead of the season, not a guarantee that its specification remained unchanged at every race.

The same report identified Fred Vasseur as team principal and introduced a pairing of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc. Those details make the launch a particularly useful historical reference point: the identity of the car, the leadership at the time and the announced drivers are all tied to a dated source, rather than inferred from a modern team profile.

## What changed underneath

Formula 1's technical analysis, published the next day, highlighted a switch to pull-rod front suspension, while retaining pull-rod suspension at the rear. That is a more precise observation than describing the SF-25 as simply “a completely new Ferrari”. It identifies a reported design decision and the period in which it was discussed.

A launch article and a technical analysis are useful complementary views, but both originate from the Formula 1 publisher group. They should not be counted as independent corroboration of every technical claim. Nor should a pre-season configuration be silently presented as the specification used throughout the year.

## What this chapter does not claim

This is a story about the February 2025 launch and the evidence available in those dated reports. It does not assert the SF-25's season-long competitiveness, a final championship result, or the team's full historical identity. Those require separate, time-specific records.
