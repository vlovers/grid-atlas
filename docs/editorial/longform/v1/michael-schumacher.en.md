## The road to Formula 1

Michael Schumacher is a German racing driver. His name is Michael Schumacher.

He was born on 3 January 1969 in Hürth, Germany. In 1990 Schumacher won the German Formula Three championship and joined Mercedes’ sports-car programme, before entering Formula 1 the following year.

## Constructor chapters

Schumacher raced for Jordan and Benetton in 1991, continued with Benetton through 1995, and drove for Ferrari from 1996 through 2006. After a break, he returned with Mercedes for 2010–2012. His Mercedes comeback ended after the 2012 season without another Formula 1 race victory.

## Championship years

His seven drivers’ world titles came in 1994 and 1995 with Benetton, followed by five consecutive championships with Ferrari from 2000 to 2004.

*Research claims: `michael-schumacher-titles`.*

## Spa and Suzuka

His Formula 1 debut was with Jordan at Spa in the 1991 Belgian Grand Prix. He returned to the circuit with Benetton in 1992 to take his first Formula 1 win. On 8 October 2000 at Suzuka, Schumacher secured his first drivers’ title with Ferrari. It ended the team’s wait for a drivers’ champion since Jody Scheckter in 1979.
