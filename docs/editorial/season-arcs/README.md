# Season Arcs · Editorial research collection

**Current package:** [Volume I](title-deciders-volume-01.en-uk.md) (1976, 1988, 2021, 2025) + [Volume II](title-deciders-volume-02.en-uk.md) (2007, 2012, 2016). Seven distinct three-act EN/UK manuscripts; **zero newly approved or published historical stories**.

## Why this exists

A season page should answer **why a championship mattered**, not just count calendar rounds and archive fields. These manuscripts follow turning points, people, teams, race outcomes and consequences, with a dramatic entry, chronological narrative and a route to another season. They deliberately do not duplicate the unfinished driver/constructor editorial PRs #274 and #289.

The seven pieces form a connected first reading trail:

1976 Hunt–Lauda → 1988 Senna–Prost → 2007 Räikkönen–Hamilton–Alonso → 2012 Vettel–Alonso → 2016 Rosberg–Hamilton → 2021 Verstappen–Hamilton → 2025 Norris–Verstappen–Piastri.

**2025 fact check:** official final standings: Norris **423**, Verstappen **421**, Piastri **410**. Verstappen came from **104 points** behind the championship leader after Zandvoort to finish two points behind Norris. Do not claim he won the 2025 title. Official standings: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2025/drivers ; season retrospective: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-very-proud-despite-remarkable-championship-comeback-falling-two.6aLc8om0STvgLnhgF8wBwP

## Evidence boundaries

- Every act contains citation markers resolved to narrow Formula1.com URLs below each story. A result table supports points and finishing order, not private motivation, regulatory intent or incident mechanics.
- All current external citations come from the **same publisher family (Formula 1)**. That is useful primary/promoter reporting but **not independent multi-publisher corroboration**. Obtain contemporaneous FIA decisions and independent accounts for disputed claims before approval.
- The 2021 Safety Car procedure is expressly a **separate unresolved research item**. Do not present an AI-authored legal verdict or invent a quote.
- No race-specific internal URL is claimed without resolving the pinned F1DB canonical race ID. Existing season routes are valid entry points.
- There are no new photos, race video, screenshots, AI recreations or license approvals in this package.
- These manuscripts are drafts, not approved story objects. The existing human editorial release pipeline remains authoritative.

## How to review and eventually publish

1. **History:** open each exact citation URL; check event chronology, official standings, source date, quoted point totals and claims beyond classifications. For 2021, consult the actual FIA review and relevant regulations. For 1976 and 1988, review era-specific points rules.
2. **EN and UK:** independent editors check natural language, nuance, transliteration and race terminology; no automatic AI self-approval.
3. **Crosslinks:** resolve driver, constructor, circuit and race identities from the pinned import; attach only existing canonical routes and explicit era/layout context.
4. **Deep dive:** add two or more additional source-backed chapters per story, with relevant race and team evidence, before calling the manuscript a long-form feature.
5. **Release:** export an exact reviewed revision through the existing approved editorial pipeline; no direct import of draft Markdown into public routes.
6. **Quality gate:** run python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v and the existing CI; tests check structure and citations but cannot replace a historical editor.
