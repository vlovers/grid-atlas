## Publication acceptance criteria

- Each claim backed by a source appropriate to that claim; result pages prove classifications, not architecture or motives.
- Five stories per venue must have distinct reader questions, not simply five finishing positions.
- Each Ukrainian translation checked against the English for meaning, idiom, and proper names.
- Historical configurations and corner names checked against period-specific maps; no inferred geometry.
- Media rights cleared for every image and graphic.
- Independent editorial approval recorded; `publication=blocked` remains until then.
- CI and review green on the exact merge commit; no automatic release from this audit.
