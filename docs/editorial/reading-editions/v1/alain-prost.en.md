## The constructor chapters

His Formula 1 career took him from McLaren in 1980 to Renault in 1981–1983, back to McLaren in 1984–1989, then to Ferrari in 1990–1991. After sitting out 1992, he returned with Williams in 1993.

**Evidence — `alain-prost-teams`:**
- [Alain Prost — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-alain-prost.6xkomcqkRL5K0HgmPX4zAH) — Formula 1; Renault, return to McLaren, Ferrari and 1993 Williams paragraphs

## World championships

## Dijon, a narrow margin, a return

His first Formula 1 victory came at Dijon in the 1981 French Grand Prix, driving for Renault.

**Evidence — `alain-prost-first-victory`:**
- [Alain Prost — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-alain-prost.6xkomcqkRL5K0HgmPX4zAH) — Formula 1; First Formula 1 victory paragraph

In 1984 he won seven races, but McLaren team-mate Niki Lauda secured the championship by half a point. More race wins did not by themselves decide the title.

**Evidence — `alain-prost-1984`:**
- [Alain Prost](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/heritage/formula-1/drivers/alain-prost/) — McLaren Racing; Narrative paragraph 5 (1984 championship).

Second place at Estoril in the 1993 Portuguese Grand Prix secured his fourth title. He had returned from a year away to partner Damon Hill, and announced that weekend that he would retire at the end of the season.

## Sharing McLaren with Senna

Prost and Ayrton Senna became McLaren team-mates in 1988. Their rivalry accompanied a season in which the team won 15 of 16 races, and continued after Prost moved to Ferrari.

**Evidence — `alain-prost-rivalry`:**
- [Alain Prost](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/heritage/formula-1/drivers/alain-prost/) — McLaren Racing; Narrative paragraphs 8–10 and 12 (Senna).

## An attributed assessment

Formula 1’s retrospective describes Prost’s race management as conserving brakes and tyres early, with pace held back for a later challenge. The familiar nickname “The Professor” reflects that analytical reputation.

**Evidence — `alain-prost-style`:**
- [Alain Prost — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-alain-prost.6xkomcqkRL5K0HgmPX4zAH) — Formula 1; Paragraph introducing The Professor and describing an economical driving style

His McLaren years spanned a major technical change: in 1984, the ban on mid-race refuelling increased the importance of fuel economy. McLaren’s heritage account places its TAG-powered car and Prost’s results in that context.

**Evidence — `alain-prost-context`:**
- [Alain Prost](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/heritage/formula-1/drivers/alain-prost/) — McLaren Racing; Narrative paragraph 5 (1984 refuelling).
