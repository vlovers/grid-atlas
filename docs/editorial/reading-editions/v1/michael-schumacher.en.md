## Constructor chapters

Schumacher raced for Jordan and Benetton in 1991, continued with Benetton through 1995, and drove for Ferrari from 1996 through 2006. After a break, he returned with Mercedes for 2010–2012.

## Championship years

His seven drivers’ world titles came in 1994 and 1995 with Benetton, followed by five consecutive championships with Ferrari from 2000 to 2004.

## Spa and Suzuka

His Formula 1 debut was with Jordan at Spa in the 1991 Belgian Grand Prix. He returned to the circuit with Benetton in 1992 to take his first Formula 1 win.

**Evidence — `michael-schumacher-spa`:**
- [Michael Schumacher — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-michael-schumacher.7KdX5nJlTG55vR5JQSbZ21) — Formula 1; Narrative paragraph 5 (Spa).

On 8 October 2000 at Suzuka, Schumacher secured his first drivers’ title with Ferrari. It ended the team’s wait for a drivers’ champion since Jody Scheckter in 1979.
