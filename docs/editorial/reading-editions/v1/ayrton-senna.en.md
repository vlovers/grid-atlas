## A career in motion

Senna entered Formula 1 with Toleman in 1984, then moved to Lotus for 1985. His first world title came with McLaren in 1988.

## Championship classifications

Ayrton Senna won the Drivers’ World Championship in 1988, 1990 and 1991 with McLaren.

**Evidence — `ayrton-senna-championships`:**

## Three race records

On 3 June 1984 Ayrton Senna finished second at the Monaco Grand Prix for Toleman-Hart, behind winner Alain Prost.

**Evidence — `ayrton-senna-monaco-1984-p2`:**
- [1984 Monaco Grand Prix race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1984/races/471/monaco/race-result) — Formula 1; 3 June 1984 result, position 1 Prost / McLaren TAG and position 2 Senna / Toleman Hart

**Evidence — `ayrton-senna-portugal-1985-p1`:**

## Senna and Prost: attributed context

Gerald Donaldson’s Formula 1 retrospective presents Senna’s McLaren partnership with Alain Prost as a rivalry that extended into their championship contests at Suzuka in 1989 and 1990. This is an attributed retrospective account, not evidence of either driver’s intentions.
