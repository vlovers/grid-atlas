# Four additional sourced season narratives

This content batch adds four **distinct historical season research dossiers**, with original UK/EN introductory narratives and **7 source-located factual claims**: 1976, 2008, 2012 and 2021.

Sources: Formula 1's official year-specific driver classification tables, the 2008 historical scoring retrospective, the 2008 race results, and the 2021 season retrospective. Source links and precise table/section locators live with each claim in [season-research.json](season-research.json). Official standings are distinguished from raw summed race results. The 2021 Belgian GP half-point note is treated as event-specific.

These four subjects are **research drafts, not independently approved or publicly published articles**. The source passages should be checked again during the independent editorial review; source links alone do not constitute human signoff. No copyrighted article prose or imagery is copied.
