# Eleven Formula 1 official-result cross-checks (research only)

The eleven canonical F1DB event identities have now been compared to Formula 1's public race-results pages. This is a **second dataset** and a useful independent data-source cross-check, not independent human editorial approval. The linked pages provide date, winner, constructor and laps; event-level links also provide venue and podium. Where only the season index was consulted, the venue comes from F1DB and is explicitly **not** claimed to be separately verified by that page.

## Verified at event-page granularity
- Williams — 1979 British GP: Regazzoni (Williams Ford), 68 laps; Arnoux second, Jarier third. [Official race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1979/races/415/great-britain/race-result)
- Mercedes — 1954 French GP: Fangio (Mercedes-Benz), 61 laps; Kling second, Manzon third. [Official race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1954/races/129/france/race-result)
- Spa — 1950 Belgian GP: Fangio (Alfa Romeo), 35 laps; Fagioli second, Rosier third. [Official race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1950/races/98/belgium/race-result)
- Suzuka — 1987 Japanese GP: Berger (Ferrari), 51 laps; Senna second, Johansson third. [Official race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1987/races/525/japan/race-result)
- Interlagos — 1973 Brazilian GP: Fittipaldi (Lotus Ford), 40 laps; Stewart second, Hulme third. [Official race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1973/races/315/brazil/race-result)
- Imola — 1981 San Marino GP: Piquet (Brabham Ford), 60 laps; Patrese second, Reutemann third. [Official race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1981/races/425/san-marino/race-result)

**Historical caution:** race result pages do not verify historical layout geometry or constructor legal continuity. The F1DB constructor IDs and Formula 1's displayed team names are different namespaces. Do not automatically translate an engine suffix into a constructor identity.
