# Five bilingual pilot chapter manuscripts

The [chapter drafts](chapter-drafts.json) expand the five [source-located research pilots](pilot-research.json) into **11 chapter sections**, each with original English and Ukrainian prose and explicit atomic claim IDs. They are intentionally brief, fact-led editorial openings, not full biographies or completed long-form historical investigations.

The chapter audit verifies that every source-located pilot claim is referenced and every chapter has both locales. It does **not** establish source independence, verify page locators, or perform human approval. It is unsafe to publish these manuscripts as approved articles.
