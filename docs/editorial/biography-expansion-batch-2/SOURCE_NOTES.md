## How evidence is used

F1DB supplies the pinned statistical comparison. Formula 1 historical profiles
supply narrative context; Gerald Donaldson is credited where the page identifies
him. Contemporary FIA reporting supports Vettel’s Sepang team-order incident.
Named engineer testimony supports driving-style passages. Team retrospectives
and sponsor biographies remain interested accounts, even when useful as primary
participant testimony. Multiple pages from the same publisher are one declared
group. Group counts do not prove independence, truth or complete corroboration.

Precise locators are in each dossier and repeat identically across locales.
Human reviewers must open the whole passage, assess every assertion in its
paragraph, consider contrary evidence and check the translation. The empty
review sidecars deliberately record none of those actions as completed.
