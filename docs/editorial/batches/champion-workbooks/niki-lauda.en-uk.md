### niki-lauda-1975-standings

The official 1975 drivers' championship classification lists Niki Lauda first for Ferrari on 64.5 points, ahead of Emerson Fittipaldi on 45.

- [Formula 1: 1975 Drivers' Standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1975/drivers) — 1975 Drivers' Standings, positions 1 and 2, driver/team/points columns (classification points, not raw per-race sum)

### niki-lauda-1976-return

Niki Lauda returned at the 1976 Italian Grand Prix, six weeks after his German Grand Prix accident, and finished fourth.

- [Formula 1: Niki Lauda — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-niki-lauda.2Ye7VYWdXwhb97ZqLyxvX) — Hall of Fame paragraph starting 'Six weeks later', documenting the Italian Grand Prix return and fourth place.

### niki-lauda-1982-mclaren

Lauda returned to Formula 1 with McLaren in 1982 after leaving the championship in 1979.

- [Formula 1: Niki Lauda — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-niki-lauda.2Ye7VYWdXwhb97ZqLyxvX) — Hall of Fame passages describing his 1979 retirement in Canada and 1982 McLaren signing.
