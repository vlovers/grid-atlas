### jackie-stewart-brm-debut

Stewart joined the BRM Formula 1 team in 1965 and drove for it for three seasons.

### jackie-stewart-safety-advocacy

Stewart publicly campaigned for improved Formula 1 safety measures, including barriers, run-off areas and better medical facilities.

- [Formula 1: Jackie Stewart — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-jackie-stewart.18715uErEG7LS83UDkeqP4) — Safety reform paragraphs on barriers, run-off areas and travelling medical support.
