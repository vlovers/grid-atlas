## Source claim register / Реєстр джерел

### lewis-hamilton-titles

Lewis Hamilton won the Formula One Drivers’ World Championship in 2008, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020.

- [Formula 1: The beginner’s guide to the F1 Drivers’ Championship](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-f1-drivers-championship.53MjXJzTDxQnfxfoCLnxNZ) — Historical champion paragraph listing Hamilton's seven championship years

### lewis-hamilton-opening

Hamilton’s 2007 race record opens with third place in Australia for McLaren Mercedes. By the Canadian round on 10 June, the same classification archive records him as the winner.

- [Formula 1: Lewis Hamilton — 2007 race classifications](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2007/drivers/LEWHAM01/lewis-hamilton) — Australia 18 March P3 and Canada 10 June P1 rows

### lewis-hamilton-2008

The final 2008 drivers’ standings place Hamilton first with 98 points and Felipe Massa second with 97. This one-point margin belongs to the official championship classification, not to an archive-derived start count.

- [Formula 1: 2008 Drivers’ Standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2008/drivers) — Final drivers’ classification rows 1–2

### lewis-hamilton-brazil

At the Brazilian Grand Prix on 2 November 2008, the official result lists Hamilton fifth for McLaren Mercedes. Read that race classification alongside the final championship table to keep the two kinds of result distinct.

- [Formula 1: Lewis Hamilton — 2008 race classifications](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2008/drivers/LEWHAM01/lewis-hamilton) — Brazil, 2 November, position 5, McLaren Mercedes row

### lewis-hamilton-mercedes

The 2013 race classification lists Hamilton with Mercedes, including a victory in Hungary on 28 July. These records anchor the Mercedes chapter without supplying the terms of a driver contract.

- [Formula 1: Lewis Hamilton — 2013 race classifications](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2013/drivers/LEWHAM01/lewis-hamilton) — 2013 team column and Hungary 28 July P1 row

### lewis-hamilton-2020

Hamilton heads the official 2020 drivers’ table with 347 points for Mercedes, ahead of Valtteri Bottas on 223. This is a season-specific classification, not a claim about all-time records.

- [Formula 1: 2020 Drivers’ Standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2020/drivers) — Final classification rows 1–2: Hamilton 347, Bottas 223

### lewis-hamilton-verstappen

The 2021 final table records Verstappen first on 395.5 points and Hamilton second on 387.5. Those totals describe a close championship outcome; they do not by themselves explain the decisions at the final race.

- [Formula 1: 2021 Drivers’ Standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2021/drivers) — Final classification rows 1–2: Verstappen 395.5, Hamilton 387.5
