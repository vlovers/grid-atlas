### fernando-alonso-birth-location

Formula 1's historical profile places Fernando Alonso's birth on 29 July 1981 in Oviedo, Asturias, Spain.

- [Formula 1: Fernando Alonso — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-fernando-alonso.2Ig4mBh2FaPMgAMjI0bBF9) — Opening biographical paragraph beginning 'Fernando Alonso Diaz ... was born on 29 July, 1981, in Oviedo'

### fernando-alonso-early-f1

Alonso made his Formula 1 debut with Minardi in 2001 and joined Renault as a test driver in 2002 before racing for the team in 2003.

- [Formula 1: Fernando Alonso — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-fernando-alonso.2Ig4mBh2FaPMgAMjI0bBF9) — Career section covering Minardi, Renault testing and the 2003 race seat.

### fernando-alonso-first-win

Alonso earned his first Formula 1 Grand Prix victory at the 2003 Hungarian Grand Prix driving for Renault.

- [Formula 1: Fernando Alonso — Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-fernando-alonso.2Ig4mBh2FaPMgAMjI0bBF9) — Paragraph on pole positions in Malaysia and Hungary and first Grand Prix victory.
