# 2008: Lewis Hamilton — a season at two scales

**RESEARCH DRAFT · not approved for publication**

## The championship classification

The Formula 1 [2008 Drivers' classification](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2008/drivers) lists **98 points** for Lewis Hamilton and **97** for Felipe Massa. These are official classification values, not recomputed totals from imported race-result rows.

## One race as a doorway

The [2008 driver results](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2008/drivers/LEWHAM01/lewis-hamilton) record Lewis Hamilton in position **5** at the Brazil Grand Prix on 2 November. One result helps anchor the chronology, but does not by itself explain the race's tactics, title mathematics or wider causes.

## Reading two kinds of evidence

Final championship standings and individual race classifications answer different questions. Historical scoring rules, dropped results and, in later eras, sprints prevent an unqualified assumption that summing every imported result row reproduces the official championship total.
