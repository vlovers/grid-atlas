# Estoril: second place, fourth title

**RESEARCH DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED**

At Estoril on 26 September 1993, Alain Prost crossed the line second in the Portuguese Grand Prix for Williams-Renault. Michael Schumacher won in a Benetton-Ford, 0.982 seconds ahead; Prost's team-mate Damon Hill finished third. This was the weekend Prost secured his fourth drivers' championship. The race result illustrates how a title-winning moment does not always require winning that day's Grand Prix. The mathematical championship clinch should be corroborated using the contemporary standings, rather than treated as a conclusion derived from this one result.
