# Spa: a first victory

**RESEARCH DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED**

On 30 August 1992, Michael Schumacher drove a Benetton-Ford to victory in the Belgian Grand Prix at Spa-Francorchamps. The official record lists 44 laps and a winning time of 1:36:10.721; Nigel Mansell finished second, 36.595 seconds behind, with Riccardo Patrese third. The result gives the story a clear anchor, one year after Schumacher's Formula 1 debut at the same circuit. It does not, by itself, establish the strategic or weather-related explanation for the margin. Those details belong to a separately sourced race narrative.
