## Gaps and priorities

1. Add deterministic evidence/coverage reports and unpublished research dossiers
   alongside #84. Source URLs alone are insufficient: require precise locators,
   access dates, source provenance and explicit unresolved questions. Start with
   Schumacher and Prost within the existing cohort; leave other drafts intact.
2. Human claim and locale review remains a real gate. No automation may fabricate
   a reviewer or promote an AI draft. Current article status vocabulary is
   `draft → in_review → published/withdrawn`; it has **no approved state**, immutable
   revision history, authenticated approval or enforced sequential transitions.
3. Source-row edits do not invalidate claim reviews in migration 0007. Independent
   reviewer identity is procedural, not authenticated. Public reads cannot prove
   every sentence is covered by a linked claim. These are separate publication
   hardening work, not reasons to build a parallel CMS or publish this batch.
4. #84 writes generated packets to its output path directly; rerunning preparation
   over a manually enriched packet risks overwriting it. Keep raw generation and
   enriched outputs separate. The complementary attachment tool must reject
   conflicting content, preserve existing fields and have repeat-run tests.
5. Reconcile stale README/architecture/API design prose with actual schema before
   implementing database draft ingestion. Keep stable external claim IDs and
   content revisions in the next importer design; current numeric database IDs
   alone do not provide an idempotent dossier mapping.
