# Historical turn identity — seven-route acceptance gate (EN/UK)

**3 November 2026 · PR #395 · research-only / не для публікації**

This checklist is an editorial acceptance protocol, **not** a claim that any historical apex has been independently verified. It complements the existing 17 count observations and map-provenance audit; it does not repeat BATCH_01/02 or create turn records.

## Required evidence for an individual historical corner

1. Identify the **race year and exact configuration** before considering a bend number or name. Keep alternate-year and modern layouts separate.
2. Record a **dated primary or period map** with page/figure reference and evidence of direction of travel. A catalogue entry for an unseen drawing is *not* a map inspection.
3. Establish a **geometric crosswalk** from the map's bend to a stable feature; distinguish a numbered bend, a named feature and an unnumbered research segment.
4. Corroborate the feature with a **second independent document** when available. Two fields from one publisher's page count as one source, not two.
5. Write a specific **EN and UK explanation**, with uncertainty and source attribution. A translation of an unverified claim remains unverified.
6. Record image author, origin, license, attribution, share-alike and modification requirements **per asset**. Linking to a map does not license copying it.
7. Keep `human_approval: false` and `publication_allowed: false` until historical identity, copy and media rights receive independent approval.
