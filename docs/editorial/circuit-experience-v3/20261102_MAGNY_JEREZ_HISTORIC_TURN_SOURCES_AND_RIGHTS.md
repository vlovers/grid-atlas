# 2026-11-02 — Magny-Cours and Jerez historic F1 turn guides

Both guides are candidate editorial data, not independently approved or published. No copied maps, photographs or new diagram overlays have been introduced.

## Magny-Cours — 2003–2008 F1 layout (magny-cours-3, 17 turns)

The venue's numbered circuit plan, turn guide and layout history support a 17-turn lap. Nürburgring (T7–8), Imola (T12–13) and Complexe du Lycée (T16–17) are shared names. T2, T6, T10 and T11 remain unnamed. The operator documents changes at Château d'Eau and Lycée in 2003; do not apply this guide to magny-cours-1 or magny-cours-2.

- Numbered plan: https://www.trophee-endurance.fr/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Circuit_PlanCircuit.pdf
- Venue corner guide: https://www.circuitmagnycours.com/en/guide-decouvrez-le-circuit-de-nevers-magny-cours/
- Venue history: https://www.circuitmagnycours.com/en/histoire-du-circuit/
- F1 2004 four-stop account: https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/france-2004-how-ferrari-stole-victory-from-renault-with-a-secret-4-stop-plan.7yXeHIzXx8e8JEyBC401ET.7yXeHIzXx8e8JEyBC401ET
- Official 2008 classification: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2008/races/832/france/race-result

Review boundary: the numbered plan is newer than 2008. The 2003–2008 layout is supported by venue history, but a 2008 period FIA numbered map remains to be checked.

## Jerez — 1994–1997 F1 layout (jerez-2, 15 turns)

The 1994 Senna chicane and the earlier Sito Pons redesign separate the 1997 F1 lap from the 1986–1990 layout. F1DB records 15 turns for jerez-2, while modern motorcycle maps can count 13. The venue identifies Dry Sack as the 1997 Schumacher–Villeneuve collision location. Later motorcycle corner names are deliberately withheld where period numbering is uncertain.

- Venue history: https://circuitodejerez.com/en/circuito/
- F1DB historical SVG (Jules Roy, CC BY 4.0): https://github.com/f1db/f1db/blob/v2026.17.0/src/assets/circuits/white-outline/jerez-2.svg
- Historical 1994–2003 map (SimLola661, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked, not copied): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Circuito_de_Jerez_1994-2003.png
- Official 1997 result: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1997/races/670/europe/race-result

Review boundary: 15 is the F1DB historical corner count, not an independently recovered FIA 1997 numbered corner map. Period turn-number/name mapping needs independent verification before publication. Later names such as Jorge Lorenzo and Dani Pedrosa must not be retroactively assigned to 1997.
