# Adelaide Grand Prix 1985–1995: sixteen individual EN/UK research corners

The F1 route is the 3.780-km sixteen-turn course, NOT the later 3.219-km Supercars course. The period layout is linked at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adelaide_(long_route).svg (Will Pittenger, CC BY-SA 3.0, no image copied). The street-by-street route and named features are described at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelaide_Street_Circuit. The 1995 classification is https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1995/races/637/australia/race-result.

**Research boundary:** the map establishes the original GP outline but does not supply a contemporaneous FIA numbered 1995 apex chart. The named sectors Senna Chicane, Banana Bend, Stag Turn (explicitly T9 in the source), Brewery Bend, Brabham Straight, Britannia hairpin and Racetrack Hairpin are discussed in sequence, but every number-to-apex assignment remains provisional. The 2017 relocation of modern T7/T8 is NOT applied to 1995. All canonical name fields are null; aliases are candidate labels only; all sections are historical_numbering_unverified. The 1994 and 1995 race stories are sourced from official F1 classifications. No new images are embedded. The linked map is CC BY-SA 3.0, not interchangeable with the F1DB SVG CC BY 4.0 licence.
