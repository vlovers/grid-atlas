## Explicit historical conflicts and boundaries

- **Argentina 1995:** official Formula1.com classification lists Hill winning after **71 laps**, with Alesi **+7.136s**. Motor Sport's period classification lists **72 laps**, with Alesi **+6.407s**. These are incompatible published records; the reason is **not established**. Do not merge them or silently choose one. Sources: https://www.formula1.com/en/results/1995/races/622/argentina/race-result and https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/may-1995/22/argentine-grand-prix-2/ .
- **Madring 2026:** FIA classification shows Antonelli first, Verstappen **+4.351s**, Norris **+5.089s**. Thus **0.738s** is Norris's gap to Verstappen, not the winning margin. The VSC pit-window explanation comes from separate contemporary reporting, not the classification. Sources: https://www.fia.com/news/f1-antonelli-wins-spanish-grand-ahead-verstappen-vsc-costs-norris and https://www.reuters.com/sports/formula1/antonelli-stretches-lead-madrings-first-f1-winner-2026-09-13/ .
- **Aida 1995:** Grandprix.com and Autosport differ on the number of fresh tyre sets available to Schumacher (four versus three). Keep the number unresolved; the race classification cannot resolve tyre inventory.
- Race classifications support results, lap counts and gaps as recorded by the chosen archive. Period reports and other independent sources are required for accidents, overtakes, strategies, named corners, track alterations and causal explanations.
