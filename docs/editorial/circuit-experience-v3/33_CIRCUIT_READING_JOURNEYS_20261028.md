**Scope:** all 33 circuits missing five-story research dossiers in the audited F1DB v2026.17.0 coverage. This is a consolidated reader-facing **first-chapter manuscript and research-question batch**, **not** the missing five stories per circuit, independently fact-checked publication, layout review, or rights clearance. Original source-linked short entries are reproduced here only as the basis for continued editorial work. No new historical claim is verified by this compilation.

**Why this document exists:** give editors one readable 33-circuit package instead of 33 disconnected cards. Each chapter has a distinct reader question, a UK/EN title and opening passage when the existing source provides one, and provenance. Monaco and Spa require separate sourced history blocks and are intentionally not filled with invented dates.
