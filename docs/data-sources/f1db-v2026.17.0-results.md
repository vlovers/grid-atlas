## Validation summary

- Coverage: 1950 through 2026 inclusive, with **zero missing season years**.
- Eight configured cross-collection checks executed: **zero detected orphan references**.
- No duplicate IDs reported in collections exposing `id`, though composite-key records require additional checks.
- No null rows reported.
- The original 37 warnings were JSON Schema documents incorrectly considered collections; these were **false positives**. The audit script now skips `.schema.json` and includes a regression test.
- The checksum verifies agreement with the published checksum list; it does not establish rights or historical truth.

## Open gaps

Full JSON Schema validation, composite-key uniqueness, every foreign-key reference, event-level race-result consistency, historical lineage semantics, licensing/brand rights and editorial provenance **remain unverified**.

**Decision:** enough information exists to design a minimal, source-provenance-first staging schema; not enough to claim that all F1DB historical facts are independently verified.
